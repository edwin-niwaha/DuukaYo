# Offline cash checkout

An online sign-in verifies active memberships and stores JWT credentials in Expo SecureStore. The app grants up to 24 hours of offline checkout access after online membership verification. Reconnect to renew. Revoked permissions are enforced by Django on every upload; a disconnected device cannot learn about revocation immediately. Shared devices should use OS lock screens and individual cashier accounts.

SQLite uses WAL and a composite user/business/branch scope for both catalog and sale queue. A cash sale receives a UUID and is inserted before the app clears the cart or shows a receipt. Cash always follows the durable queue, even with connectivity. Restarts reopen the same database. Synchronization runs on entering a workspace, app foreground, a 30-second timer, and explicit refresh. Repeated uploads carry the identical persisted payload. The server serializes mutations by business and checks UUID plus canonical payload hash before creating any sale, payment or movement.

States: pending (not acknowledged), synchronized (server receipt saved), needs-review (server price/stock conflict or permanent input rejection). Transport errors, auth failures and throttling keep pending sales. A rejected record stays available to share as a recovery record and retry unchanged; it is never deleted. A successful server response with review reasons is retained and must not be re-uploaded. Signing out preserves local records but clears visible data and credentials. Sign in as the same authorized user to recover them. Uninstalling or clearing app storage destroys local data: synchronize and retain receipts before device maintenance.

Offline receipts preserve the price actually charged and the cost from the cached catalog. Changed cost snapshots are also flagged at upload. Online sales reject stale prices. Offline stock conflicts are recorded, can make physical stock negative, and are flagged for the owner to reconcile with a reasoned stock adjustment. Offline discounts are prohibited; cashiers may not invent connected discounts. Cached stock is last known stock, not a guarantee, and does not reserve anything. Owner reports explicitly include only synchronized sales; heartbeat pending counts are best-effort and cannot identify unknown disconnected sales.

Storefront uses physical stock minus reserved stock minus a per-shop safety buffer (default 2). Every order is provisional until accepted. Pending reservations expire after 30 minutes; accepted/preparing/ready reservations persist until completion or cancellation. Run Celery beat continuously. Completion deducts physical stock once; cancel/expiry releases the reservation. An offline POS sale can still consume stock that an online customer reserved. Staff must reconcile physically and cancel/refund manually if necessary; this architecture does not promise global stock certainty during partitions.

Manual MTN/Airtel entries require connectivity in the mobile UI. They are payment records, never provider verification. FCM and reservation operations need connectivity. Receipt sharing works through the native share sheet; printer adapters require a named-device implementation and hardware validation.
